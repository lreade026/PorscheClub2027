The story

Turns out, winning championships doesn't make racing any cheaper.

Between 2022 and 2025, I won three championships, including a European GT3 title. The results were there; unfortunately, the budget didn't quite keep up!

In 2027, our family team, Wolf Motorsport, is getting back on track in the top class of the Porsche Club Championship. It's the first step on the Porsche ladder, and the beginning of a bigger ambition: making it all the way to Le Mans.

I've got unfinished business, a team behind me, and plenty more racing to do.

I'd love you to be part of the journey.

The plan
01 / 05 · 2027

Porsche Club Championship

A well-established and highly competitive championship within the Porsche racing community. We'll be racing our 997 Carrera S across seven rounds around the UK, including our home event at Brands Hatch.

It's the perfect place to kick-start our Porsche journey, build momentum and establish a strong relationship with the Porsche racing community.

The target? Get out there, put the work in and bring home another championship.

Our first step into Porsche racing. Hopefully the first of many.

02 / 05

Porsche Sprint Challenge GB

The next rung on the Porsche ladder, racing modern Cayman GT4 machinery in an official Porsche one-make championship.

A chance to build momentum, sharpen the competition and take another step towards Carrera Cup.

The next step up.

03 / 05

Porsche Carrera Cup GB

Racing Porsche 911 GT3 Cup cars on the BTCC support package, in front of packed circuits and a national TV audience on ITV4.

Big grids, big crowds and plenty of eyes on the action.

A national stage. A serious platform.

04 / 05

International GT racing

The next step is taking our racing programme beyond the UK, competing in top-level GT3 championships across Europe.

A European title is already part of the story. Now we're looking to write the next chapter.

05 / 05

Le Mans

The 24 Hours. The big one. The reason this whole journey started.

Every step from here is about getting closer to that grid. Partners who come on board now aren't just supporting one season; they're joining us at the beginning of something much bigger, with first opportunity to grow alongside us as we climb the Porsche ladder.

The dream is Le Mans. This is where we start.
